### Hi, I'm synchrium 👋

Small, free apps with Japanese names — made to make your Mac (and Windows) a little nicer.<br>
日本語の名前の、小さくて無料のアプリを作っています。

| App | What it does | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Nakami**](https://github.com/synchrium/Nakami) 中身 "contents" | Look inside an archive before you extract it | Mac |
| [**Mitame**](https://github.com/synchrium/Mitame) 見た目 "how things look" | Sharp, Retina-like text on non-4K monitors | Mac |
| [**Hayami**](https://github.com/synchrium/Hayami) 早見 "a quick look" | Copy a screenshot's image, file, or path in one keystroke | Mac & Windows |
| [**Mimoto**](https://github.com/synchrium/Mimoto) 身元 "identity" | See where your apps came from, and remove them the right way | Mac |
